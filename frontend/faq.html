Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, get in touch.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool listed as "runs on-device" does its work inside your browser using WebAssembly and Canvas. Your file is read into memory, processed, and handed back as a download. It never crosses the network. You can verify this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab, and run any tool — you will see no upload.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes, once installed. The app stores its whole toolkit — including the OCR engine and the encryption library — on your device. After that, the app makes no network requests of its own. It only reaches the network when you press "Check for updates".

How do I install it?

Open PDFLove in Chrome, Edge or another Chromium browser and use the Install button in the header, or the install icon in the address bar. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari's Share → Add to Home Screen. On Mac Safari, File → Add to Dock. It then behaves like any other installed application.

Is there a file-size limit?

There is no upload, so there is no upload limit. The real limit is your device's available memory. The app estimates the memory a job needs and warns you before starting something that is likely to fail.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no login, no email address and no profile. Nothing to sign up for.

Will my output have a watermark?

Never, unless you added one yourself with the watermark tool.

Can I unlock a PDF I have the password for?

Yes — the Unlock PDF tool removes a password you can already supply. It is for documents you own or are authorised to access. It is not a password cracker and will not open a document you do not have the password for.

What are the "server-assisted" tools?

A handful of jobs genuinely need heavyweight native software — full-fidelity Office conversion, deep Ghostscript compression, PDF/A, and chat-with-document. Those are an optional, open-source backend you run yourself. They are clearly labelled, and the app never looks for a backend automatically: you have to point it at one.

Is my data used to train anything?

No. We never receive your files, so there is nothing to train on. The on-device AI models are pre-trained and read-only — they do not send your text or images anywhere.
