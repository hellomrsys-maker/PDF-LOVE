Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

PDFLove processes your files entirely on your own device. For the great majority of tools, nothing about your file — not its contents, not its name, not even the fact that you used a tool — is ever sent anywhere.

What we do not collect

  • File contents or filenames handled by on-device tools.
  • Accounts. There is no login, so there is no email address, password or profile.
  • Location, contacts or device identifiers.
  • Analytics or tracking identifiers by default.

What is stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data in your browser's local storage, purely so it behaves sensibly between visits. It never leaves your device and we cannot read it:

  • Which tools you pinned and recently used.
  • An optional backend URL, if you set one.
  • An offline licence key, if you have one.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.

Optional features that do use the network

  • Server-assisted tools. Only appear when you have configured a backend, and only send the specific file you submit to that specific tool. If you self-host the backend, the file goes to your own server and never to us.
  • Update checks. Only when you press "Check for updates".
  • Advertising on the free web version, where enabled. Ads never appear before or during processing, and never see your file. An offline licence removes them.

Children

PDFLove is a general-purpose document utility with no account system and collects no personal information, so it holds no data about children or anyone else.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, export and erasure apply to personal data a service holds about you. We hold none: there is no account and no server-side copy of your documents. If you have used a self-hosted backend, any data lives on your own server, under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date above will change and the summary will say what moved. Questions: privacy@pdflove.co.in.
