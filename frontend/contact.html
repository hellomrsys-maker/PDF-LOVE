Before you write in

A good number of questions are already answered on the FAQ and in the guides. If a tool failed on a specific file, the error message shown in the app usually names the cause — including it in your message saves a round trip.

Please never email us a confidential document. We designed the whole product so we would not receive your files; sending one by email undoes that. Describe the problem instead, or send a file you do not mind sharing.
