Turn a video into a GIF without a watermark

Most free online GIF makers upload your clip to their server, slap a watermark on the result, and push you toward a paid tier to remove it. Dockbench converts video to GIF entirely in your browser instead.

The 3-step answer

  1. Open Dockbench and choose Video to GIF.
  2. Drop in a video clip and pick your trim range, size, and frame rate.
  3. Dockbench encodes the GIF on your device (using gifenc, a pure-JavaScript encoder) and hands you the file — nothing uploaded, nothing watermarked.
Open Dockbench and try it →

Cloud GIF makers are convenient but usually watermark free results or cap clip length. Dockbench's on-device conversion has no watermark and no daily limit, though very long clips will take longer since your own device is doing the encoding work.
