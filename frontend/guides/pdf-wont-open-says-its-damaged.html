PDF won't open / says it's damaged

A PDF that suddenly won't open — or that your reader says is "damaged" or "corrupted" — often still has recoverable content. Before giving up on it, it's worth trying a repair pass.

The 3-step answer

  1. Open Dockbench and choose Repair PDF.
  2. Drop in the file that won't open normally.
  3. Dockbench attempts to rebuild the PDF's internal structure and recover whatever content is intact, all in your browser.
Open Dockbench and try it →

This isn't magic — if the underlying data is truly gone, no tool (cloud or local) can bring it back. But for the common case of a broken internal structure (an interrupted download, a bad save), a repair pass often recovers the file. Doing this on-device means a possibly-sensitive document isn't uploaded to a random "PDF repair" website in the process.
