Why can't I select text in my scanned PDF?

A scanned document is really just a photograph saved as a PDF — there's no real text in it, only pixels. That's why you can't select, search, or copy anything from it. The fix is OCR (optical character recognition), which reads the image and adds an invisible, searchable text layer underneath.

The 3-step answer

  1. Open Dockbench and choose OCR — Make Scans Searchable.
  2. Drop in your scanned PDF or photo. A real OCR engine (Tesseract, the same open-source engine used by desktop scanning software) runs directly in your browser — no file upload, works offline once loaded.
  3. Download a new PDF that looks identical but now has selectable, searchable, copyable text underneath every word.
