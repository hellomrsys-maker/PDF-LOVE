How to black out private info in a PDF

Drawing a black box over text in a PDF viewer often doesn't actually remove the text underneath — it's still there if someone selects or copies it. Real redaction removes the underlying text entirely, not just hides it visually.

The 3-step answer

  1. Open Dockbench and choose Search & Redact.
  2. Search for what you want to remove (Dockbench can auto-find patterns like SSNs, emails, and phone numbers) or mark specific regions yourself.
  3. Dockbench removes the underlying text and image data — not just paints over it — and gives you a new PDF, all on your device.
Open Dockbench and try it →

This is exactly the kind of task where uploading to a cloud service is the wrong move — the whole point of redaction is removing sensitive information, not sending it to a third party first. Dockbench redacts locally, so the sensitive data only ever exists on your own device.
