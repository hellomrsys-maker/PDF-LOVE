How to remove a password from my own PDF

If you know the password on your own PDF and just want to stop being asked for it every time you open the file, you can strip the encryption entirely — no need to send the file anywhere to do it.

The 3-step answer

  1. Open Dockbench and choose Unlock PDF.
  2. Drop in your PDF and enter the password you already know.
  3. Dockbench decrypts it using a real WebAssembly build of qpdf, in your browser, and gives you back an unlocked copy.
Open Dockbench and try it →

Cloud "remove PDF password" tools require uploading a file that, by definition, you were protecting for a reason. Dockbench decrypts it locally — the password and the file both stay on your device.
