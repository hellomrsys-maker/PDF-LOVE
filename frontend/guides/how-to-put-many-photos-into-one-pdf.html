How to put many photos into one PDF

Whether it's receipts for an expense report, pages of a scanned document photographed with your phone, or a set of screenshots — turning several images into one PDF makes them easy to send and keep in order.

The 3-step answer

  1. Open Dockbench and choose Images to PDF.
  2. Drag your photos in — you can reorder them before converting.
  3. Download one PDF containing every image, in the order you set, done entirely in your browser.
Open Dockbench and try it →

Your phone's built-in "print to PDF" trick works for one photo at a time but is clumsy for many. Cloud converters work, but require an upload for something that's often personal (receipts, ID photos, medical scans). Dockbench keeps every photo on your device the whole time.
