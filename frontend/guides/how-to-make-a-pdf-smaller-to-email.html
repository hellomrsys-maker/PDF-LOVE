How do I make a PDF small enough to email?

Most email providers cap attachments around 20–25 MB. If your PDF is bigger, here's the free, private way to shrink it — nothing leaves your device.

The 3-step answer

  1. Open Dockbench and choose Make PDF Smaller (or just type what you need into the assistant — see the button below).
  2. Drop your PDF in. Dockbench re-encodes the images inside it and strips the redundant data — entirely in your browser, using the same WebAssembly engine (qpdf) real desktop software uses.
  3. Download the smaller file. It never leaves your device, so there's nothing to wait on and nothing to worry about.
Open Dockbench and try it →

Cloud tools (iLovePDF, Smallpdf) do the same job but require uploading your file to their servers first, and most cap you at 1–2 free compressions a day before asking for a subscription. Dockbench compresses on your device, so there's no upload wait and no daily limit.
