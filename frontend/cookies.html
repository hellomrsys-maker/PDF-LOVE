Cookies

Last updated: 2 August 2026

PDFLove sets no tracking cookies. There is no account system, so there is no session to track and no advertising profile to build.

What is actually stored

The app uses your browser's local storage — not cookies — for a few preferences. This data never leaves your device and is not sent with any request:

  • pdflove.favs — tools you pinned.
  • pdflove.recents — tools you used recently.
  • pdflove.apiBase — an optional self-hosted backend address.
  • pdflove.licenseKey — an offline licence key, if you have one.

The installed app also keeps a cache of its own files so it can run offline. That cache holds the application itself, never your documents.

Advertising

Where advertising is enabled on the free web version, an ad network may set its own cookies when an ad is displayed, under its own policy. Ads never appear before or during processing and never have access to your files. An offline licence removes advertising entirely.

Removing it

Clearing site data for pdflove.co.in in your browser removes every item above. The app will simply start fresh with no pinned tools.
