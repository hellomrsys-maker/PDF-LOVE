Why the free tier is actually free

Not a trial, and not limited-until-you-pay. On-device tools cost us nothing per use — your computer does the work, and we only pay to host static files. There's no per-request bill for us to pass on, so there's no meter on you. The paid tiers exist because server processing and commercial integration genuinely do cost money.
