Why this page exists

Rule 3(2) of India's Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 requires a named Grievance Officer with working contact details and published timelines. The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules, 2025 require a readily available way to raise a complaint about personal data. This page is both.

What you can raise

  • Anything about your personal data, including a request to know, correct or erase it.
  • Content on this site you believe is unlawful, infringing or wrong.
  • An advert you saw here that was misleading, offensive or malicious — we want to know; a bad advert is our problem to fix, not yours to tolerate.
  • Anything the Service did that you believe it should not have.

How to file, and what happens

Email grievance@pdflove.co.in with enough detail to identify the problem — the page or tool involved, what happened, and what you would like done. You do not need a lawyer, a form or any particular wording.

If we do not satisfy you

You are entitled to escalate, and you do not need our agreement:

  • India — the Data Protection Board of India, for complaints about personal data under the DPDP Act, 2023.
  • EEA or UK — your national data protection supervisory authority.
  • Elsewhere — your local consumer or data protection regulator. Ask us and we will point you to the right one.
