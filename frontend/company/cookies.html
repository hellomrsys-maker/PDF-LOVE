Cookies & Advertising

We set no cookies. This page explains the small amount we keep in your browser, what advertising partners do, and how to say no.

We set no cookies of our own. Not one — not for analytics, not for sessions, not for preferences. There is no cookie banner outside Europe because outside Europe there is nothing of ours to consent to.

1. What we store on your device

The app keeps a few values in your browser's local storage. This is not a cookie: it is never attached to a network request and never sent anywhere. We cannot read it; only your browser can.

WhatWhy
Pinned toolsKeeps your starred tools at the top.
Recently usedPuts the last few tools you used within reach.
Licence keyOnly if you bought one, so you are not asked again.
Backend addressOnly if you configured your own self-hosted backend.
Exchange ratesA short-lived cache, only if you used the Currency Converter.

Clear it any time through your browser's "clear site data" or private browsing. Nothing breaks; the tools simply forget your preferences.

2. Advertising cookies

Advertising partners set their own cookies when an advert is displayed — typically to cap how often you see the same advert and to detect click fraud. These are theirs, not ours, and are governed by their policies.

Adverts never appear inside the tool workspace, and no advert script loads while a file is being processed. That is enforced in the code and verified by an automated test on every deploy, not merely promised here.

3. What happens where you are

Where you areWhat happens
EU, EEA, UK, SwitzerlandA consent banner appears before any advertising cookie is set, as the ePrivacy Directive and GDPR require. Decline and you still get the full tools — adverts become non-personalised or absent. Reopen the banner any time to change your answer.
CaliforniaWe do not sell or share personal information. We honour the Global Privacy Control browser signal automatically.
Everywhere elseContextual advertising, matched to the page rather than to you.

4. Refusing advertising cookies anywhere

  • Block third-party cookies in your browser settings — the tools are unaffected, because they use none.
  • Turn on Global Privacy Control, which we honour.
  • Use the ad networks' own opt-outs at optout.aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Install the desktop or Android app, or use the site offline — with no connection there are no adverts at all, and every on-device tool still works.
