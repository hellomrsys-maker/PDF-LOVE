Scan Document

A phone photo of a page is not a scan: it is taken at an angle, lit unevenly, and surrounded by whatever your desk looks like. This finds the page edges, corrects the perspective, and flattens the lighting — the job a dedicated scanner app does, without installing one.

Your file never leaves this device. Pick a file and watch the Network tab — nothing fires while it is being processed. No upload, no account, no daily limit.

How to use Scan Document

  1. Point your camera at the page, or choose a photo you already took.
  2. Check the detected corners and drag any that landed wrong.
  3. Pick an output: colour, greyscale, or high-contrast black and white.
  4. Add more pages if you have them, then download the finished PDF.

What to know

Corner detection looks for the strongest quadrilateral in the frame, so a page on a contrasting surface is found reliably while a white page on a white desk may need manual adjustment. The perspective correction maps your four corners onto a true rectangle, which is what removes the trapezoid look. High-contrast mode is the right choice for printed text: it drops the paper to pure white and typically cuts the file size by an order of magnitude compared with a colour photo.

Common questions

The corners were detected in the wrong place.

Drag them onto the page corners yourself. Detection is a starting point, not the final answer.

Should I use colour or black and white?

Black and white for printed text — smaller and sharper. Colour only if the page has photographs or coloured markings that matter.

Can I scan several pages into one PDF?

Yes. Capture each page in turn; they are collected in order and saved as a single document.
