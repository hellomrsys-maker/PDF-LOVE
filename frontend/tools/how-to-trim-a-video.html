How do I trim a video?

Cut to the part you want, keeping the audio.

Your file never leaves this device. Pick a file and watch the Network tab — nothing fires while it is being processed. No upload, no account, no daily limit.

The short answer

Cut to the part you want, keeping the audio.

What is actually going on

There are two ways to cut a video and the difference is worth a minute of your attention. A stream copy keeps the existing compressed data and only changes where playback starts and stops. It is nearly instant and loses nothing — but it can only cut at keyframes, so your cut may land a fraction of a second from where you set it. Re-encoding cuts exactly where you asked, at the cost of decoding and recompressing everything, which takes time and loses a little quality. For trimming dead air off the front of a recording, the fast path is right. For a precise cut in the middle of speech, take the slower one.

Step by step

  1. Open Trim Video and load the file.
  2. Set the in and out points on the timeline.
  3. Choose the fast copy, or exact cutting if the frame matters.
  4. Export and play the result through the joins.

Common questions

Why did my cut land slightly off?

A fast trim snaps to the nearest keyframe. Exact cutting re-encodes and lands where you asked.

Is the audio kept?

Yes, trimmed in step with the picture.
