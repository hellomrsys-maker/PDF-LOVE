Convert PDF to Text

Turn a Portable Document Format into a plain text file (.txt) without sending it anywhere. The conversion runs on your own device.

Your file never leaves this device. Pick a file and watch the Network tab — nothing fires while it is being processed. No upload, no account, no daily limit.

How to convert PDF to Text

  1. Open the PDF to Text tool.
  2. Choose your PDF file, or drag it onto the page.
  3. Adjust the options if you want to — defaults are sensible.
  4. Download the Text result. It saves straight to your downloads folder.

Why this one is different

Just the words, in reading order. Most online converters upload your file to their server, process it there, and hand you a download link — which means your document sits on someone else's machine under a retention policy you have to take on faith. This one does the work in the browser you are already running.

The practical differences: there is no daily limit, because each conversion costs us nothing. There is no upload wait, because there is no upload. And it keeps working with the network off.

Common questions

Is this PDF to Text converter really free?

Yes, with no daily limit and no account. Your device does the work, so there is no per-use cost to pass on.

Is my PDF file uploaded to a server?

No. It is read by your browser, converted in memory, and saved back to your device. You can confirm this in the Network tab of your browser's developer tools.

Will there be a watermark on the Text file?

No watermark is added.

What is the largest file I can convert?

In a browser, around 2 GB — browsers cap a single buffer at roughly that. The desktop app streams from disk and handles far larger files.
