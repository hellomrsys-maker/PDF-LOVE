Why install it rather than use the website?

The browser version is genuinely good, but a browser tab cannot hold a file larger than about 2 GB — that is a hard limit of the JavaScript engine, not something any web tool can work around. The installed app streams documents from disk instead, so its capacity is your free disk space.

Verifying your download

Every release publishes SHA-256 checksums alongside the installers. On macOS and Windows the installers are code-signed; your OS will show the publisher before installing. If a download is unsigned or the checksum does not match, do not run it — get it from the official releases page.

What the app sends

Nothing about your files, ever — not their contents, not their names, not the fact that you opened one. The installed app makes exactly one kind of routine request: an update check that sends a version string and receives a signed manifest. Everything else is local.
