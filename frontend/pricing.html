Why is it free?

Because the expensive part of a PDF service is the servers that process files, and PDFLove does not have that cost — your browser does the work. Running costs are limited to serving a few megabytes of static files, which is covered by optional, non-intrusive advertising on the web version. Ads never appear before or during processing, and never on an installed copy with an offline licence.

No dark patterns. There is no watermark you have to pay to remove, no daily counter, and no "upgrade to finish this file" wall. If a tool is listed, it works.
