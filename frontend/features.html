What PDFLove does differently

Most online PDF tools upload your file to a server, process it there, and ask you to trust them with it. PDFLove does the work inside your browser instead — so the question of trust never comes up.

🔒

Your files stay put

Merging, splitting, compressing, OCR, encryption — all of it runs on your own CPU and memory. Nothing is uploaded, so nothing can leak, be retained, or be scanned.

✈️

Works with no internet

Install it once and the whole toolkit — including the OCR engine and the WebAssembly encryption library — is stored on your device. Turn the Wi-Fi off; it still works.

🚫

No sign-up, no daily limit

No account, no email, no "2 files per day, upgrade for more". Every tool is available every time you open the app.

💧

No forced watermarks

Output is your document, unmarked. The only watermark that ever appears is one you added yourself with the watermark tool.

📦

No file-size ceiling

There is no upload, so there is no upload limit. The practical ceiling is your own device's memory, and the app warns you before it gets close.

🧠

AI that runs locally

Summarising, translation, speech-to-text and image captioning use models that download once and then run on your device — not a metered cloud API.

The full toolbox

83 tools, grouped the way you would actually look for them:

  • Organize — merge, split, rotate, reorder, crop, extract pages and images, remove blank or duplicate pages, N-up, headers and footers, page numbering.
  • Optimize — compress, repair, OCR a scan into searchable text, grayscale.
  • Convert — PDF to and from Word, Excel, PowerPoint, JPG, text, Markdown, HTML and PDF/A.
  • Edit & sign — a real page editor, signatures, stamps, watermarks, highlighting, form filling, search-and-redact.
  • Security — AES-256 password protection, unlocking PDFs you own, sanitising hidden scripts and metadata, flattening.
  • Images & video — compress, resize, convert, remove backgrounds, passport photos, ID cards, invoices, GIF creation, trimming, audio extraction.
